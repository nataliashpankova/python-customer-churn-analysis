# 📊 Key Insights

- Customers with short tenure have significantly higher churn probability
- Monthly contract customers are more likely to churn than yearly subscribers
- High monthly charges increase churn risk
- Certain customer segments show consistent churn patterns

# 💡 Business Recommendations

1. Introduce loyalty programs for long-term customers
2. Offer discounts or bundles for monthly subscribers
3. Identify high-risk customers early using behavioral patterns
4. Improve customer experience for high-value segments
